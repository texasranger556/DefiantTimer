Location & clock

Every command also sets the timer's clock and location. The timer uses the location to work out sunset and sunrise for Dusk to Dawn.

Two decimal places is plenty. Western longitudes are negative. You can copy coordinates from a long-press in Google Maps.

Turn this off if your area doesn't observe daylight saving time.

Before you send
  1. Plug the cable from the phone's headphone jack (or a USB-C audio adapter) into the timer.
  2. Turn media volume all the way up. Turn off Dolby, equalizer or "adaptive sound" effects.
  3. Keep this page open until the send bar finishes.
